It serves as a structured knowledge base where I organize paper summaries, literature reviews, research ideas, reproductions, and notes on topics related to my undergraduate research and future graduate studies.

## Current Focus Areas

- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Hallucination & Faithfulness
- Claim Verification
- Confidence Calibration
- Evaluation Metrics
- Multilingual NLP

## Repository Structure

- **Foundations** – Core NLP and transformer concepts
- **Topic Folders** – Research papers and notes organized by area
- **Professor Notes** – Research summaries and insights from labs of interest
- **Research Ideas** – Potential project ideas, observations, and future work

> This repository is continuously updated as I read new papers, reproduce experiments, and refine my understanding of modern NLP research.
